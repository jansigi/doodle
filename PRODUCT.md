# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two groups with very different contexts:

- **Band members** (volunteer musicians and singers of a worship band) open a
  shared link, usually on their phone from a group chat. They enter their name,
  pick their roles and answer Ja / Vielleicht / Nein for every celebration of
  the semester. They visit once or twice per semester and must get through it
  in a minute without instructions.
- **The admin** (the person who plans the band) works on a laptop. They create
  the doodle, configure the lineup, watch the answers come in, close the
  doodle, review and adjust the generated plan and export it as PDF or Excel.
  Sessions are longer and denser, with large tables.

## Product Purpose

Semester planning for a worship band: collect availability, then generate a
fair plan (everyone plays about equally often, no back-to-back weeks where
possible, personal wishes respected) that the admin can fine-tune and share.
Success means the band answers quickly, the admin trusts the generated plan and
only needs a few manual edits.

## Brand Personality

Clean, modern, calm. Simple but with a quiet confidence, like a carefully built
tool rather than a weekend project. Reference: Linear, for its calm surfaces,
fine lines instead of heavy boxes, precise typography and care in the small
details. Tone of copy: friendly, short, Swiss German spelling (ss, no ß).

## Anti-references

- The over-the-top "vibe-coded" look: gradients everywhere, glassmorphism,
  oversized hero sections, emoji decoration, every block in its own card.
- Generic admin templates with heavy shadows, thick borders and loud colors.
- Anything that makes a simple availability form feel like a complex app.

## Design Principles

1. **Quiet by default, loud only where it matters.** Status colors (Ja,
   Vielleicht, Nein, warnings) carry meaning, everything else stays neutral.
2. **Structure through type and spacing, not boxes.** Hierarchy comes from
   size, weight and rhythm. Borders are hairlines, cards are the exception.
3. **Fast on the phone, dense on the laptop.** The participant flow is touch
   first and needs no explanation. The admin views may be information dense
   but must stay scannable.
4. **Every detail finished.** Consistent radii, focus states, hover states,
   empty states and transitions. Small polish is what makes it feel premium.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline: text contrast at least 4.5:1 (including
placeholders), full keyboard operation with visible focus, touch targets of at
least 44px on mobile, status never conveyed by color alone, and reduced motion
respected for every animation.
